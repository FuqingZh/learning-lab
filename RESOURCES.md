## Source policy

- Prefer primary scientific, standards, and repository-contract sources.
- Record the checked date and applicable version, commit, or contract status.
- Treat source-repository documents as case authority for that repository, not
  as substitutes for independent scientific or computing foundations.
- Keep community wisdom separate from normative contracts and measured facts.
- Re-check drift-prone product links and current authority before reuse.
