# Annotation evidence boundaries are understood

The learner can distinguish transferred eggNOG annotations from direct Pfam
domain evidence and explain their different causal chains and row grains. The
learner also separates the sequence-search engine, annotation tool, SeqEvi
adapter contract, and immutable external runtime identity.

The remaining profile-HMM misconception has been corrected. The learner now
explains that a Pfam profile HMM is estimated from positions in a multiple
sequence alignment, contains position-specific amino-acid probabilities and
transitions among match, insertion, and deletion states, and does not contain
protein atomic coordinates.

## Evidence

In unaided retrieval, the learner identified `ToolRuntimeDigest` as the
EvidenceKey coordinate affected by a changed external execution environment.
After corrective teaching, the learner accurately restated the source,
contents, and non-structural nature of a Pfam profile HMM.
