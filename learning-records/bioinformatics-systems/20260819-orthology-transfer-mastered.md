# Orthology-aware functional transfer is understood

The learner can classify orthologous and paralogous relationships from the
speciation or duplication event that separates a gene pair rather than from
species membership or sequence-similarity thresholds. The learner understands
that paralogs may occur in different species and that a very low E-value
supports non-random sequence similarity but does not establish orthology or
functional equivalence.

The learner can also reconstruct the eggNOG-mapper inference boundary after
sequence mapping: a seed match leads into an orthologous group, precomputed
phylogeny refines the evolutionary relationship, taxonomic scope constrains
eligible donors, and one-to-one, one-to-many, or broader ortholog selection
controls annotation transfer reliability and coverage.

## Evidence

In unaided retrieval on 2026-08-19, the learner restated E-value as the expected
number of equally strong or stronger chance matches in the current search,
rejected function and orthology as conclusions from E-value, and named
phylogeny, taxonomic scope, and ortholog-type selection as the post-seed eggNOG
evidence needed to choose annotation donors.
