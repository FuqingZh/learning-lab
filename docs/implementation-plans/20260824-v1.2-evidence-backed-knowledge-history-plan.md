# v1.2 Evidence-backed knowledge history implementation plan

## Outcome

Add an auditable historical layer to Learning Lab so a learner can understand
how a professional concept emerged, changed meaning, and entered modern
practice from real documents rather than a fabricated teaching story.

The existing knowledge graph remains the authority for present-day concepts.
Historical dossiers become a separate source contract. Learning state remains
append-only evidence of learner capability, and the generated site becomes a
projection of all three contracts.

## Non-goals

- Do not invent dialogue, motives, incidents, or a single heroic inventor.
- Do not treat the earliest source found in a search as proof of priority.
- Do not rewrite modern concept definitions as historical claims.
- Do not turn dates, names, and anecdotes into the mastery target.
- Do not bulk-generate a history page for every concept before the pilot is
  reviewed.
- Do not change `normalized_graph_v1` or the learning-state event schema.
- Do not require a database, network service, or JavaScript dependency.

## Authority boundaries

| Source | Authority |
| --- | --- |
| `concepts/` | Present-day professional definition and typed relationships |
| `histories/` | Evidence-backed terminology, problem, formalization, adoption, revision, and critique history |
| `lessons/` | Teaching sequence that may consume concepts and histories |
| `case-labs/` | Current repository application evidence |
| `learning-state/sessions/` | Append-only learner observations |
| `maps/` and `site/` | Reconstructable projections only |

A history dossier owns its links to concepts and lessons. Concept files do not
gain a mirrored `histories` field; consumers derive reverse membership from the
history projection.

## Historical evidence rules

1. Every milestone claim has at least one directly linked source.
2. A claim about an actor's motive requires a contemporary primary source or
   explicit support from a scholarly historical analysis.
3. `first`, `invented`, `coined`, and similar priority claims require sources
   that specifically establish priority; otherwise use bounded wording such as
   `documented in` or `used by`.
4. Terminology origin, problem origin, formalization, adoption, revision, and
   popularization remain distinct milestone kinds.
5. Primary sources establish what a document said or did. Later standards and
   professional documentation establish later adoption, not undocumented
   original intent.
6. Disagreement, incomplete evidence, or competing lineages is recorded
   explicitly. Missing evidence fails closed and is not completed by AI prose.
7. Historical material must preserve the terminology gate: a historical use
   does not automatically establish the present professional meaning.

## Source contract

One dossier lives at `histories/<stable-id>.md`. The pilot schema is:

```yaml
---
schema_version: 1
id: idempotency-history
title: History of idempotency
summary: How an algebraic property entered HTTP semantics and retry-oriented API design.
concepts:
  - idempotency
lessons: []
tracks:
  - scientific-ai-platforms
milestones:
  - id: peirce-1870
    year: 1870
    month: null
    day: null
    kind: terminology
    actors:
      - Benjamin Peirce
    claim: Benjamin Peirce documented idempotent as an algebraic property in Linear Associative Algebra.
    evidence_basis: primary-source
    sources:
      - url: https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/titleinfo/6674068
        title: Linear Associative Algebra
        publisher: National Academy of Sciences
        role: primary
        kind: monograph
---
```

Required top-level fields are `schema_version`, `id`, `title`, `summary`,
`concepts`, `lessons`, `tracks`, and `milestones`. A dossier must link at least
one concept or lesson.

Milestone kinds are `terminology`, `problem`, `formalization`, `adoption`,
`popularization`, `revision`, and `critique`. Source roles are `primary` and
`scholarly-secondary`. Source kinds are `monograph`, `paper`, `standard`,
`archive`, and `professional-documentation`. Every milestone also declares
`evidence_basis` as `primary-source`, `scholarly-secondary`, or `mixed`.
`primary-source` requires at least one primary source;
`scholarly-secondary` requires at least one scholarly secondary source; and
`mixed` requires at least one source of each role.

`year` is required. `month` and `day` are nullable integers; a day requires a
month. Consumers sort by `(year, month-or-zero, day-or-zero, milestone-id)` and
must not display an omitted month or day as January or the first day of a
month.

The Markdown body uses these headings:

```markdown
## Historical setting
## What the sources establish
## What the sources do not establish
## Development
## Modern boundary
```

The body can explain evidence and uncertainty, but it cannot introduce an
uncited milestone claim that is absent from frontmatter.

## Normalized history v1

`scripts/build-knowledge-history.py normalized-data` emits:

```json
{
  "schema_version": 1,
  "dossiers": [{
    "id": "idempotency-history",
    "title": "History of idempotency",
    "summary": "...",
    "path": "histories/idempotency-history.md",
    "concepts": ["idempotency"],
    "lessons": [],
    "tracks": ["scientific-ai-platforms"],
    "milestones": [{
      "id": "peirce-1870",
      "year": 1870,
      "month": null,
      "day": null,
      "kind": "terminology",
      "actors": ["Benjamin Peirce"],
      "claim": "...",
      "evidence_basis": "primary-source",
      "sources": [{
        "url": "https://...",
        "title": "...",
        "publisher": "...",
        "role": "primary",
        "kind": "monograph"
      }]
    }]
  }]
}
```

All paths are repository-relative and all lists are deterministically ordered.
Unknown extension metadata is either recursively JSON-normalized or rejected;
`json.dumps(default=str)` is prohibited.

## Validation

The history builder rejects:

- duplicate YAML keys;
- missing or unknown fields in the v1 contract;
- unstable or duplicate dossier and milestone IDs;
- nonexistent concepts, lessons, and tracks;
- empty dossier membership;
- invalid calendar components;
- duplicate or non-HTTPS source URLs;
- unknown milestone/source enums;
- an `evidence_basis` that is inconsistent with the milestone's source roles;
- missing required Markdown headings;
- non-JSON-compatible extension values; and
- nondeterministic ordering.

Structural validation does not claim that a source supports a sentence,
proves priority, or records an actor's motive. The how-to guide and review
checklist own that human judgment.

## Pilot content

### Idempotency

Audit and document at least these distinct stages without collapsing them:

1. Benjamin Peirce's documented algebraic terminology in 1870.
2. HTTP/1.1 method semantics in RFC 2068 in 1997.
3. Current HTTP semantics connecting idempotent methods to retry after a lost
   response in RFC 9110.
4. Modern application-level idempotency tokens or keys in mature API practice.

The dossier must explicitly state that an algebraic property, an HTTP method
property, and an application-level deduplication mechanism are related but not
identical.

### E-value

After the canonical-concept pilot is stable, add one lesson-linked dossier for
the E-value material in
`lessons/bioinformatics-systems/alignment-score-bit-score-evalue.md`. Begin
with Karlin and Altschul's 1990 statistical-significance problem, distinguish
the original ungapped local-alignment assumptions from later tool practice,
and avoid treating current BLAST output conventions as the original problem.

This second dossier proves the contract can support a lesson topic without
prematurely promoting it into `concepts/`.

## Tutor behavior

Update the repository tutor skill so a dossier can supply one authentic
historical increment:

1. present the documented time, actor, technical conditions, and source;
2. state only what was knowable in that historical setting;
3. ask the learner to predict a next step without attributing that prediction
   to the historical actor;
4. reveal what the source actually proposed;
5. contrast the historical meaning with the modern concept boundary; and
6. reuse `explain-back` or `fresh-case-transfer` for evidence rather than
   adding a date-memory mastery state.

The tutor must never turn an inferred motive or invented scene into historical
fact. If a dossier is absent or incomplete, it teaches from the concept and
lesson contracts without manufacturing history.

## Site projection

The self-contained Pages site embeds normalized history beside `GRAPH` and
`LEARNING_STATE` as a separate `HISTORY` object.

- Add a clear `关系图 / 历史谱系` mode switch.
- Selecting a concept derives matching dossiers through dossier membership.
- Render a chronological timeline with date precision preserved.
- Show actor, milestone kind, claim, source role, and direct source link.
- Distinguish primary sources from scholarly secondary sources.
- Provide a visible uncertainty/no-history state.
- Do not turn milestones into dependency-graph concept nodes.
- Recall mode may expose the documented historical problem while hiding the
  modern definition and relationships.
- Preserve global, track, case-lab, focal, Today, Continue, and Recall behavior.
- Preserve offline/self-contained rendering, keyboard interaction, reduced
  motion, and safe HTTPS external links.

Visual polish follows the information architecture. Do not redesign an empty
shell before the historical data and interaction contract exist.

## Verification and acceptance

Focused verification includes:

```bash
python3 scripts/build-knowledge-history.py validate
python3 scripts/build-knowledge-history.py normalized-data
python3 tests/knowledge-history/test_validation.py
python3 tests/knowledge-map/test_site_render.py
```

The canonical completion gate remains:

```bash
bash scripts/check-structure.sh
git diff --check
```

Completion additionally requires:

- two subprocess reads of normalized history are byte-identical;
- the generated site's embedded history exactly equals canonical normalized
  history;
- a browser smoke selects Idempotency, switches to historical lineage, opens
  a primary source link safely, and preserves Recall behavior;
- every pilot milestone is manually checked against the linked source;
- the final generated `site/index.html` is freshly inspected; and
- the pre-implementation learner-progress commit remains separate from all
  v1.2 structural changes.

## Implementation status

Implemented on 2026-08-24. All four slices are complete: the repository has a
strict normalized-history contract, two source-audited pilot dossiers,
historical teaching rules in the tutor skill, and a self-contained Pages
projection with relationship/history switching, dossier deep links, Recall,
source links, and a lesson-only E-value route. The two dossiers are a reviewed
vertical slice, not a claim that every concept already has a historical file.
