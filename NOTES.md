# Teaching Notes

- Preferred language: Chinese.
- Preferred depth: exhaustive when useful; bottom-up explanations from biology,
  bytes, operating systems, databases, and distributed systems are welcome.
- Preferred structure: define the layer and the problem before introducing the
  abstraction or command that solves it.
- Use concrete SeqEvi examples, counterexamples, small tables, and causal
  diagrams. Explicitly separate scientific identity from operational identity.
- End bounded lessons with an unaided retrieval or application question.
- Do not add glossary entries merely because a term was presented; wait for the
  learner to use it correctly.
- Review cue: revisit sequence identity and the full evidence key before moving
  from caching into Store concurrency.
